Magnetic Pendulum: Initial Position, Damping, and Energy Loss

An RK4 numerical simulation for exploring how initial conditions, damping, magnetic-force strength, and time step affect two-dimensional motion.

Simulation Controls

Suggested test: Run a case using Δt = 0.002 s, then repeat using Δt = 0.001 s or 0.0005 s under exactly the same physical settings. Compare the path, final region, and energy-balance difference.

Physics Model, Numerical Method, Assumptions, and Limitations

Governing Equations

\[ m\frac{d^2x}{dt^2}=F_x-c\frac{dx}{dt} \] \[ m\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}=F_y-c\frac{dy}{dt} \] \[ r_i=\sqrt{(x-X_i)^2+(y-Y_i)^2+h^2} \] \[ F_x=\sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{A(X_i-x)}{r_i^3} \] \[ F_y=\sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{A(Y_i-y)}{r_i^3} \]

Energy Model and Damping Check

The force model above is consistent with the simplified potential energy U = −Σ A/rᵢ. The mechanical energy is E = K + U, where K = ½m(vₓ² + vᵧ²).

Linear damping removes energy at the rate dE/dt = −c(vₓ² + vᵧ²). This simulation approximates the cumulative dissipated energy:

\[ W_d(t)=\int_0^t c\left(v_x^2+v_y^2\right)\,dt \] Ideal energy relation: \[ E(t)+W_d(t)\approx E(0) \]

Chosen Values

  • The magnets form a triangle at approximately (−0.065, −0.040), (0.065, −0.040), and (0.000, 0.070) m.
  • The vertical separation is h = 0.018 m. This keeps rᵢ nonzero and prevents the simplified force expression from becoming singular at a magnet location.
  • The selectable RK4 steps are Δt = 0.002 s, 0.001 s, and 0.0005 s. Smaller Δt can improve convergence, but it does not automatically guarantee correctness, stability, or physical realism.

Assumptions

  • The pendulum is represented as a point particle moving in a horizontal plane.
  • It starts from rest unless the source code is modified.
  • Gravity is balanced by support and does not directly accelerate horizontal motion.
  • The damping coefficient c is constant and damping is proportional to velocity.
  • There are no external energy inputs after release.
  • Motion stops when the speed stays below a selected threshold for a specified duration, or when maximum simulation time is reached.

Important Limitations

  • The magnetic-force model is a simplified educational approximation. It is not an exact description of real magnetic interactions.
  • The basin map uses a coarser calculation than the displayed main trajectory, so its classification can change if its resolution, simulation duration, or Δt changes.
  • Small initial-position changes may or may not produce different final regions. Use the comparison mode and basin map to test this behavior for chosen parameters.
  • At very low damping, the simulated pendulum may not settle before the maximum time.
